Welcome
Whether you're preparing for interviews, joining international calls, writing emails to clients, or
simply want to sound more confident at work, this book gives you 300 real, commonly used
expressions organized the way they actually come up in business life — by situation, not just
alphabetically.
How each entry works:
Expression — the phrase itself, in natural business use.
Meaning — a clear, plain-English explanation.
Example — a realistic sentence from a real workplace context.
Register — how formal or casual the expression sounds.
Similar to — an alternative phrase you could use instead.
Tip: Don't try to memorize all 300 at once. Pick 5–10 a week from the chapters most relevant to
your role, and start using them in real emails and meetings right away.
